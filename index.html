Frequently Asked Questions

Are these prints safe for younger kids?

Yes! We use non-toxic, biodegradable PLA+ plastic derived from cornstarch. However, toys with smaller parts are recommended for children ages 4 and up with adult supervision.

How does Hood River local delivery work?

When you check out, select "Local Gorge Delivery". We will text or email you to coordinate a convenient drop-off time right to your porch or meeting spot in Hood River or nearby.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept peer-to-peer transfers via **Venmo** and **Zelle**, as well as secure credit/debit card checkout.
